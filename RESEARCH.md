The photo viewer showed January and February day labels without an independently exposed year. The website therefore omits event dates and client names. ## Pricing and terms

No independently verifiable current menu, price list, guest minimum, capacity, deposit, cancellation or rescheduling policy was found. The site requests a tailored quote and current menus. It makes no availability, price or allergy-safety guarantee. Guest count is a visitor's planning input, not a claimed service capacity. No awards, statistics, testimonials or years in business were invented.

## Design and visitor journey

The site uses an editorial event-portfolio direction: dark ink, copper, ivory, oversized serif typography, a narrow vertical rail, asymmetrical photography and service chapters. Visitors explore real work, save inspiration, select services and prepare a personal event inquiry with original photo links. This gives the catering business a distinct experience from a resort booking website.

## Technical scope

The website is static and works without a backend. Inspiration can be stored locally when browser storage is available. An inquiry is prepared for user review; Facebook, email, clipboard and download options are user initiated. No live booking, payment or automatic lead submission is implemented. A later online collection service would need appropriate delivery, data handling and privacy provisions.
